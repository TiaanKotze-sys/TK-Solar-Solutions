Hybrid Solar System

Best of Both

Connects to solar panels, a battery bank, and the Eskom grid simultaneously — automatically prioritising the most cost-effective source. You maintain power regardless of grid availability.

  • Automatic switching between solar, battery, and grid
  • Uninterrupted power during extended outages
  • Earn credits by exporting surplus energy to the grid
  • Most cost-efficient solution over the long term
  • Ideal for homes, home offices, and small businesses
